TOEIC Capture Auto Post

TOEIC Capture Auto Post is a personal, single-user automation tool. It is used only by its owner to publish his own TOEIC study videos to his own YouTube channel, @toeic_capture, as YouTube Shorts on a schedule.

What the app does

Using the YouTube Data API v3, the app:

  • uploads the owner's own short study videos to his own channel;
  • verifies that the authorized account owns the target channel before uploading;
  • lets the owner delete his own test uploads, correct metadata on his own videos, and schedule his own videos for publication.

Data use

The app is used by one person only — its owner. It accesses only the owner's own YouTube account and videos. It does not collect data about any other person, does not serve other users, and does not share any data with third parties.
