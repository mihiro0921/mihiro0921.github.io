Privacy Policy — TOEIC Capture Auto Post

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Overview

TOEIC Capture Auto Post (the “app”) is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account @toeic_capture, a TOEIC study content account run by a university student in Japan. Its sole purpose is to publish the operator’s own pre-made TOEIC study short videos to the operator’s own TikTok account on a schedule, using TikTok’s official Content Posting API. The app is used by the operator only; it is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. How the app accesses TikTok

The app accesses the operator’s TikTok account only through TikTok Login Kit OAuth, with the scopes user.info.basic and video.publish. The scope video.upload is also requested for draft uploads.

3. Data handled

The data handled by the app is limited to:

  • the operator’s own TikTok basic profile information, such as display name and avatar; and
  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens for the operator’s own TikTok account.

The video files posted through the app are the operator’s own study videos, created by the operator.

No data about any other person is collected.

4. How tokens are stored and used

OAuth tokens are stored securely by the automation platform. Tokens are never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

5. Revoking access

The operator can revoke the app’s access at any time from TikTok’s settings (Settings and privacy > Security and permissions > Apps and services), or by contacting the operator.

6. Contact

Contact is via the operator’s note page: https://note.com/toeic_capture
